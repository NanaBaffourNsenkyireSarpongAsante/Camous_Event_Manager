Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

How do I purchase tickets?

You can purchase tickets directly from our website by visiting the Events page, selecting your preferred event, and clicking the "Get Ticket" button. Payment can be made via mobile money or card.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are available up to 7 days before the event. Please contact our support team with your ticket reference number.

How do I become an organizer?

Click on the "Organizer" tab in the navigation menu to access the dashboard where you can create and manage events.

Is there parking available?

Most venues offer parking facilities. Check the specific event page for parking information.
